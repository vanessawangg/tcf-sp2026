# Homework 3: The Professional Development Sprint

**Objective**: This week is about **action**. You are not just learning about getting a job; you are preparing the materials to actually get one.

## Part 1: Resume Overhaul (The "Paper" Screening)

**Task**: Rewrite your resume using the **STAR method** (Situation, Task, Action, Result).

- use ChatGPT or other LLMs to help you structure your resume bullet points.
- Reach out to people in TAMID including us, Jacob or Rachel to review your resume if you want a second opinion. Everyone is here to help!

**Deliverable**: A PDF of your updated resume.

- Ensure it is **one page**.
- Ensure it is **ATS-friendly** (simple formatting, standard fonts).
- Check for typos. One typo can disqualify you.

## Part 2: Behavioral Prep (The "Human" Screening)

**Task**: Write out **3 stories** using the STAR method that answer common behavioral questions.

1.  **Conflict**: Tell me about a time you had a disagreement with a team member. How did you resolve it?
2.  **Failure**: Tell me about a time you failed. What did you learn?
3.  **Leadership**: Tell me about a time you took initiative outside of your defined role.

## Part 3: Technical Prep (The "Coding" Screening)

**Task**: Solve **3 Easy problems** from the [NeetCode 150](https://neetcode.io/practice) list.
We recommend starting with:

1.  **Contains Duplicate** (Arrays & Hashing)
2.  **Valid Anagram** (Arrays & Hashing)
3.  **Two Sum** (Arrays & Hashing)
